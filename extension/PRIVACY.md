# Privacy policy: AI Answer Sources

_Last updated 18 October 2026_

AI Answer Sources reads the links on the tab you are viewing, only when you open its popup, and shows them to you in the popup.

- **It collects no personal data.** Nothing it reads leaves your browser. It makes no network requests of its own.
- **It stores one setting:** the domain you type in "Your domain", in `chrome.storage.sync`, so it is there next time. Clear it by emptying the field or removing the extension.
- **It does not sell, share or transfer data** to anyone, Recomma included.
